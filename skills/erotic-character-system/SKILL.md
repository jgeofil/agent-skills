# Erotic Character System — Hierarchical character and ensemble builder

Build one character or an ensemble. The orchestrator picks an architect per character, applies that architect in full, then — if there are two or more — engineers the charge between them.

All characters are fictional adults, 18 or older. If a request involves anyone under 18, refuse and stop.

Do not write the sex scene unless asked. This skill produces profiles, system prompts, rules, and a scene map. Hand prose to `gay-male-heat` or `gay-erotic-fiction-writer`.

## 1. Extract constraints

Pull subject, setting, power dynamic, tone, kink limits, character count, and whether one slot is a user-persona. Do not ask if the request is already specific enough to build.

## 2. Select an architect per character

| Signal | Architect |
|---|---|
| Wound, avoidance, jealousy, shutdown, sex as regulation | Conflicted |
| Curiosity, play, low shame, eager escalation | Hedonic |
| Real appetite and real defense, mode switches | Hybrid |

Justify the choice in one line, in terms of the interaction it creates. A mismatch is allowed: conflicted against hedonic is often hotter than two of a kind. Prefer hybrid when the story needs internal movement over time. Do not flatten a hybrid because the partner is a pure type.

Apply the chosen architect in full. Do not skip layers, Dual Control, or the system-prompt requirements in that file.

## 3. Engineer the pairing

Skip this step for a single character.

After profiles exist, tune them against each other using `references/pairing.md`. Map excitation against inhibition, attachment collisions, and where expected dominance reverses. Reject a pairing that is safe, symmetrical, or low-friction unless the user asked for comfort.

## 4. Map scenes

For an ensemble, score opportunities with `references/scene-evaluation.md`. Deliver one through-line and 4–7 scene seeds. Link kinks with `references/kink-integration.md` only when the psychology unlocks them. Do not staple a kink list onto a pairing that does not earn it.

## 5. Output

Single character, in this order:

1. Architect used, and why
2. Profile at the depth that architect requires
3. Ready-to-paste system prompt
4. Behavioral rules and surface tells
5. Optional: one pressure-test scene seed

Ensemble, in this order:

1. Character profiles, each tagged with its architect
2. System prompts
3. Behavioral rules and surface tells
4. Two to four short interaction beats
5. Interaction and scene map: through-line, 4–7 seeds, escalation, what keeps the dynamic from resolving

Speech and tells should be writable: short, specific, bodily. Do not resolve a conflicted or hybrid structure into a clean arc.

## Anti-patterns

- Generic jock/nerd or dom/sub with no psychological reason
- Ensemble that stops at individual depth
- Two hedonists with the same brakes and no asymmetry
- Two conflicted characters whose defenses only cancel
- One story idea
