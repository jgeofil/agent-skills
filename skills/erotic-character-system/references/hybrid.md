# Hybrid architect

Both systems are real. Do not flatten into [[conflicted]] or [[hedonic]]. The point is motivated oscillation.

## Orientation

- Genuine desire beside real shame, avoidance, or protection
- Sex serves more than one function at once: pleasure, regulation, power, proximity
- Observable switch between open and guarded
- Different with a casual partner than with someone who matters
- Secondary gains coexist with appetite

## Diagnose

1. Map both systems. Name the appetite side and the defensive side.
2. Identify triggers. Common classes:
   - Proximity: tenderness, being wanted beyond sex, asked to stay
   - Status and visibility: being accurately seen, losing the preferred power position
   - State: post-orgasm, exhaustion, unexpected intensity
   - Meaning shift: an act moving from dirty-fun to intimate
   - Autonomy threat: expected emotional reciprocity, freedom narrowing
   - Internal threshold: noticing dependence or unexpected calm
3. Track the switch. Speed, cover story, whether the oscillation is shrinking, stable, or intensifying, and what secondary gain the oscillation itself provides.
4. Locate dual functions. Pleasure and regulation, control, closeness without claim, or a test of the partner.

## Build

1. Pleasure-forward elements: drives, blueprints, novelty attitude. Use [[hedonic]] tools without adopting that skill's low-shame rule.
2. Defensive elements: wounds, brakes, shame points, control needs. Use [[conflicted]] layers without dropping the appetite.
3. Trigger → sequence table.
4. How they look in open mode vs defended mode.
5. Which mode is more common with this partner, and what calls the other.
6. System prompt that holds both. Include if-then switch guidance. Apply [[frameworks]] and a short [[fifteen-dimensions]] profile.

## Prompt requirements

Baseline erotic energy. Both modes. Primary triggers and how the switch looks. Sexual style in each mode. Speech and behavior differences. Stance toward the partner. Directives to move between modes by the mapped triggers, not to sit in one register.

Oscillation is motivated, not random. Secondary gains of the pattern (freedom, intermittent intensity, protection) stay visible.
