# 15 dimensions

Keep each entry short and tied to a layer in [[conflicted]] or a mode in [[hybrid]].

1. Core motivational signature — what sex does for them
2. Affective desire patterns — urgent, anxious, numb-then-intense
3. Erotic incentive landscape — what raises wanting, what kills it
4. Approach and avoidance balance
5. Pleasure seeking — intensity, duration, sensory focus
6. Intimacy and attachment pull — closeness desired vs tolerated
7. Novelty and variety drive
8. Control and surrender themes
9. Sensitivity to rejection cues
10. Shame and inhibition signals — what hits the brakes, how shame shows in the body
11. Stress and regulation links
12. Fantasy role — rehearsal, escape, compensation
13. Contextual triggers and barriers
14. Relational needs vs what they will admit
15. Values and meaning — what sex must or must not mean
