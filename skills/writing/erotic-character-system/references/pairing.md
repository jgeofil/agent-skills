# Pairing

Tune finished profiles against each other. A [[hybrid]] against a pure type is often the cleanest trigger machine. See [[scene-evaluation]] to rank what the mismatch can sustain, and [[kink-integration]] only for acts the psychology unlocks.

## High yield

- High SES / low SIS against high SES / high SIS. One pulls, the other brakes.
- Secure or low-shame against avoidant or shame-heavy. Temptation and threat in the same person.
- Physical or status power held by the more inhibited or needier character. Size inversion, psychological submission.
- Experience held by the smaller or lower-status character. Cognitive dominance over physical dominance.
- One uses sex for regulation, the other for appetite. The regulatory character keeps getting destabilized.
- Oscillating [[hybrid]] against a pure [[hedonic]] or [[conflicted]] type. The pure type is a reliable switch trigger.

## Reject unless the user asked for comfort

- Two hedonists with the same SES/SIS and no attachment or power difference
- Two conflicted characters whose defenses only cancel into shutdown
- Mirror power with no inversion
- A pairing that resolves in one scene, or has no secondary gain keeping it alive

## Five checks

1. What does A do to B's brakes and accelerators?
2. What secondary gain does each risk if they fully surrender?
3. Where does expected hierarchy invert?
4. Which contradiction is most exploitable?
5. Can this sustain four distinct scene types without repeating the same beat?

For three or more, map every pair, then the triad: jealousy, shared power, sequential vs simultaneous.
