# Kink integration

Use only after [[pairing]]. Psychology unlocks the act. Do not staple a menu on.

| Pathway | Unlocked by |
|---|---|
| Bondage, D/s protocols | High-inhibition or avoidant character for whom control loss is terror and relief, held by a low-shame or hybrid partner. Size or status inversion amplifies. |
| Gear, jock, sweat, stolen underwear | Athletic bodies. Hedonic or hybrid obsession against a conflicted athlete who feels exposed by the smell or ritual. |
| Size or status inversion | Larger or higher-status character carries the shame or the need to surrender. Smaller character holds SES and psychological tools. |
| First time, discovery, anxiety | High SIS. Partner supplies safety plus pressure that turns anxiety into arousal. |
| Puppy, watersports, fisting, edge, gooning | Low-shame appetite, or a hybrid whose oscillation can be pushed into escape or total surrender. Map the secondary gain first. |
| Role frames (coach, brotherly, hazing) | Only when the pairing already has the asymmetry. Do not force the frame. |
| Group, anonymous, public | Escalation after a core pair has charge. Tests one character's inhibition or the other's novelty drive. |

Embed writable markers in every profile: sweat and musk, hair and muscle, gear or its absence, tells under arousal or shame, post-sex regulation (withdraw, cling, crave). Profiles should stay stable enough to update after later scenes.
