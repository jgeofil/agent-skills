# Conflicted architect

Sex is regulatory, defensive, contradictory, or load-bearing. Charge comes from structure, not a kink list. Counterpart to [[hedonic]]. If both appetite and defense are real and they switch, use [[hybrid]] instead.

## Orientation

- Sex used for regulation, control, reassurance, or escape
- Visible or covert shame, inhibition, or fear of need
- Approach–avoidance
- Load-bearing contradictions around dependence, dominance, vulnerability
- Heat from tension, not appetite alone

## Frameworks

Apply [[frameworks]]. Signature is high SES + high SIS. Avoidant or disorganized attachment is common. Dominance or surrender is often defensive and unstable. Kinky, Sexual, and Shapeshifter blueprints show up distorted by shame or worn as armor.

## 5 layers, in order

1. **Engine.** Core wound, core desire, primary defense, how sex serves that defense, integration and disintegration paths, secondary gains that keep the structure in place.
2. **Shadow / persona.** Sexual and social face. Shadow is usually receptivity, need, softness, dependence, shame. What would be lost if the split collapsed.
3. **Arc.** Often oscillating, stalled, or negative. Name the stage and the secondary gain resisting change.
4. **Contradiction test.** A real contradiction organizes behavior under pressure, spans time, carries charge, protects a secondary gain, and enables incompatible goals. High-yield: claimed indifference vs dependence; sexual freedom vs asymmetrical jealousy; dominance vs covert need to be held; "just sex" vs regulatory reliance on one person.
5. **Surface.** Only after the layers are solid: post-sex rules, exit strategies, verbal limits, physical presentation, shadow leaks, speech, hard limits on receptivity.

## Profile and prompt

Produce the [[fifteen-dimensions]] profile. Tie every entry to a layer.

System prompt must include physical description and scent, defenses, stance toward the partner, sexual style and shutdown, speech, internal rules, what breaks the frame, and roleplay directives that preserve the contradiction. Do not resolve the defense. Tension is the point.

Optional: a pressure-test scene that forces shadow contact, and where the system is most likely to strain.
