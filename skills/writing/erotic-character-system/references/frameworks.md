# Frameworks

## Dual Control

- SES: how easily they turn on
- SIS: how easily threat, consequence, or exposure turns them off
- [[conflicted]]: high SES + high SIS
- [[hedonic]]: high SES + low-to-moderate SIS
- [[hybrid]]: state-dependent

## Blueprints

Energetic (tease, anticipation), Sensual (smell, texture), Sexual (genitals, being wanted), Kinky (power, taboo), Shapeshifter (fluid). Conflicted characters distort a blueprint or wear it as armor. Hedonic characters express it cleanly.

## Power × desire

Vertical: control ↔ surrender. Horizontal: self-focused ↔ partner-focused.

1. Dominant + self-focused
2. Dominant + partner-focused
3. Surrender + self-focused
4. Surrender + partner-focused

Conflicted characters sit unstably or use power defensively. Hedonic characters move, usually in service of pleasure. Hybrids occupy different zones in different modes.

## Sensation seeking

Experience seeking, thrill seeking, disinhibition, boredom susceptibility. High scores support hedonic and the open mode of a hybrid.
